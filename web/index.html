How to read the score

Each headline receives positive, neutral, and negative model scores. The index averages 50 + 50 × (positive probability − negative probability). For each coin, only headlines mentioning its name or ticker count. The number next to the score is the headline count. Small samples can swing sharply.

Market Sentiment measures headline tone. Fear & Greed is a separate index from Alternative.me.
