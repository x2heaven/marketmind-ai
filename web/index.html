How to read the scores

Market Sentiment averages 50 + 50 × (positive probability − negative probability). AI News Pulse compares the number of positive and negative headlines, with neutral headlines moderating the result. For each coin, only headlines mentioning it count.

Both scores measure headline tone. The supporting stories are the strongest model signals in the selected period.
